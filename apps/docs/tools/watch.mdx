---
title: "Watch"
description: "Find the approved claims a label change made wrong."
---

A label revision can change a dose, widen an indication or add a warning. Every approved piece that relied on the old wording is now out of date, and nothing tells you which ones. Watch does.

## Build a library

Check each approved piece with `--save`, against the label version it was approved under:

```sh
openlabel check approved/csu-banner.txt --label dupixent --label-version 53 --save
openlabel check approved/dupixent-email.html --label dupixent --label-version 53 --save
```

Each report lands next to its piece as `<file>.check.json`. It records the label version and the exact sentences every claim rests on.

## Watch it

```sh
openlabel watch approved
```

```text
Dupixent (dupilumab) · v53 (2026-02-23) to v60 (2026-04-22)
  indication §1.7, dosing §2.3 §2.4 §2.9 §2.11, warning §5.2, adverse reactions §6.1 §6.2, ...

  blast radius  2 pieces, 2 claims
  ✗ csu-banner.txt
    “DUPIXENT is approved for chronic spontaneous urticaria in patients 12 and older”  cites §1.7 ¶1, which changed
      was  DUPIXENT is indicated for ... patients aged 12 years and older with chronic …
      now  DUPIXENT is indicated for ... patients aged 2 years and older with chronic s…
    safety information  §5.2 Conjunctivitis, Keratitis, and Blepharitis changed
```

For every piece whose label has moved on, Watch fetches both versions, diffs them, and reports three kinds of impact:

| Impact | When it fires |
| --- | --- |
| Cited sentence changed | A sentence a claim cites was edited or removed. The report shows what it said then and says now. |
| Indication changed | An indication claim cites the changed indication section, or cites nothing at all. |
| Safety information outdated | The piece carries safety information and the boxed warning, contraindications or warnings changed. |

Pieces whose label has not changed are listed as current. Pieces whose label changed in ways they don't rely on are listed as unaffected.

## Running it on a schedule

`watch` exits 0 when nothing in the folder is affected and 1 when something is, so a nightly CI job can open an issue whenever a label update touches approved content.

## How the diff works

Sentences are compared section by section with a longest-common-subsequence diff. An edited sentence is shown as one change rather than a removal and an addition when the two share at least half their words. Inserting a table renumbers every later table, so "Table 23" becoming "Table 24" is not reported as a change. See [`openlabel diff`](/cli/diff) to read a diff directly.
