Open Label is a command-line tool and a Claude Code plugin for the people who write to physicians and the reviewers who sign off on it. Everything it says about a drug is traced to a sentence in that drug's FDA prescribing information, fetched from DailyMed and cited by section and paragraph.

## What it is not

Open Label drafts and flags. A person signs off. It does not approve content, it does not replace your MLR process, and every verdict links to the evidence a reviewer needs to overrule it.

It reads the label and public data only. Claims that rest on published literature, data on file or real-world evidence are outside what it can check, and it says so rather than guessing.
